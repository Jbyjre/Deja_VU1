The Deja Vu1 program on this computer is stopped or still starting. The dashboard, demo data included, comes from that program, so it has to be running.

  1. Open your Deja Vu1 folder.
  2. Double-click start-windows.bat (Windows), start-mac.command (Mac) or start-linux.sh (Linux).
  3. Leave the window that opens running. Closing it stops the dashboard.

The address is http://localhost:8000 unless you chose another port.

So this doesn't happen again: in Deja Vu1, open Settings → Desktop app and turn on Start Deja Vu1 when this computer starts.

Keep the computer running Deja Vu1 awake, leave its launcher window open, and reconnect to your home Wi-Fi or your private remote connection.
