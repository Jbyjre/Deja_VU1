# Campaign use and review

These are draft creatives for review, not published ads. The image is an
invitation to explore one useful idea. Its matching caption supplies context,
qualification and a destination; a detailed screenshot is not expected to do
all the explaining on its own.

## Choose a message and destination

| Subject | Intended job | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Fleet | Introduce the local U1 workspace | Open the simulated farm |
| Needs you | Show a concrete fault that deserves attention | Explore the attention list |
| Preparation | Explain the plate-to-preview workflow | Read the engine/setup guide |

`09-current-campaign/captions.json` includes a purpose, stage, suggested primary
text, caption, alternative and qualifications for each export. Keep the relevant
limits with the post. No physical U1 test is verified. The loop sculpture is
AI-generated conceptual brand art; it is not hardware or a manufactured print.

## Choose an export

| Suffix | Pixels | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| `-feed` | 1200 × 628 | Native horizontal feed ad or link-post artwork |
| `-square` | 1080 × 1080 | Square feeds and community posts |
| `-portrait` | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 feed posts |
| `-landscape` | 1600 × 900 | Forum/editorial images and 16:9 covers |

These are four independently arranged layouts. Do not crop one blindly into
another placement. The 4:5 images are feed assets, not dedicated Stories/Reels
exports. Check the actual placement preview before posting. The reviewed
LinkedIn specification accepts the square size; its exact recommendation is
1200 × 1200. It recommends 1200 × 628 for horizontal and lists a 5 MB file limit.
All current exports fit that file-size limit. Platform requirements can change.

## What changed visually

The previous set used one pale card template with an entire screen in a large
image box. The redesign keeps a shared mark and type family but uses a different
composition for each subject: a dimensional loop and fleet surface, a magnified
fault, a cobalt preparation view, a vermilion refusal panel, a large software twin,
and an owner-testing poster. Contrast, scale and depth establish a focal point.

Actual UI is enlarged or cropped for the point being made. Crops and illustrative
composition do not establish a real printer connection. Headline and evidence
text are typeset separately so their wording stays exact and editable.

## Evaluate usefulness

Before publishing, view the candidate at the intended feed width. Check that a
reader can identify Deja Vu1, the relevant U1 task and the next action. Inspect
qualifications and image alternatives. Ask U1 owners what they believe the
product does; a short review is qualitative feedback, not proof of ad performance.

If a campaign is later authorized, choose the outcome first. Discovery can aim
for useful demo visits; consideration for setup-guide visits; recruitment for
owners completing the watch-only signup. Compare creatives under comparable
audience, placement, destination and offer. Test a defined variable rather than
changing everything at once. Record denominators and uncertainty. A few likes or
a visually stronger poster do not establish adoption or a higher award score.

No analytics, tracking pixels, paid campaign or social posting was added here.
The private research record is outside the repository; the PR explains the main
sources and the reasoning. Current design is ready for owner review, not a claim
of measured marketing effectiveness.
